# Changelog

## The deeper, more immersive hill (October 2026)

### Depth
- **1,368 new hands-on activities.** Every one of the 12 subject rooms now has a chapter scroll with six activities for each of the 19 chapters. Tick them off as you go; progress saves on the device.
- **114 new discussion questions.** Six more per chapter in the Hearth Room, on top of the original two.
- **19 "go deeper" extensions** for older children, one per chapter, from writing a character essay to researching real lake villages, riddles in Old English, and how stories show war.
- **57 cross-subject quests**, three per chapter, each joining several rooms (for example baking, maths and drama for a real tea party). Each quest has a checklist and door buttons to the rooms it uses.
- **The chapter ledger** in the Hall shows every chapter against every room, shaded by how much is done. Tap a square to jump straight there.
- **Printable chapter plans.** One button prints a whole chapter: summary, all questions, the go-deeper task, the quests and all 72 activities with tick boxes.

### Immersion
- **The Lamplighter**, an original narrator who walks ahead with a lantern, greets each room, hints at sealed doors, notices keys and curios, and nudges you towards the next chapter. He can read his words aloud using the browser's own voices.
- **Sounds of the hill.** Every room has its own soundscape, all made live in the browser with no recordings: hearth crackle, wind, streams, a lapping lake, cave drips, birdsong, a song thrush, crickets, chimes, coins and a ticking clock. Sounds crossfade as you walk between rooms. Off by default, with a mute button and a volume slider.
- **Candlelight and a carried lantern.** A gentle flickering glow at the edges of the page, and an optional lantern that lights the room around your pointer.
- **A hillside palette** taken from photos of real round-door homes: grass and moss greens, weathered blue and green doors, a marigold window frame, timber, brick, cream plaster and sky. A turf fringe and timber beam frame the passage bar.
- **Doors, scrolls and maps.** Chapter work unrolls on a wooden-handled scroll. Every room now ends with painted arched doors to its neighbouring rooms, and a button to unroll the labyrinth map.

### Four new hidden rooms (seven secret rooms, eight keys and sixteen curios in all)
- **The Barrel Cellar**, beneath the Kitchen, opens to a riddle. Five barrel logic puzzles of rising difficulty, a float-or-sink experiment, and a barrel volume calculator.
- **The Echo Gallery**, behind the Little Stage, opens to a riddle. A clap-it-back listening game, a monologue maker for any character and feeling, a voice ladder and tongue-twisters.
- **The Mapmaker's Attic**, above the Map Room, opens to any four keys. A grid-reference hunt on an original valley map, a distance ruler using Pythagoras, and a compass-rose quiz.
- **The Last-Light Door**, off the Old Hall of Kings, opens only to every key. A printable family journey book, end-of-study reflections, and a "There and Back Again" scroll for finishing all 19 chapters.
- Each new room has its own painted plate, a hidden curio and a new lore key (Cellar, Echo and Compass).

### Kept as it was
- Everything still saves locally, with no accounts, and works on phones, tablets and computers.
- Still static files for GitHub Pages: `index.html`, `css/deeper.css`, `js/*.js` and one content file per chapter in `content/`.
- All art, riddles, puzzles and activities are original. Nothing is quoted from the book. One copywork line that echoed a well-known line from the author's later work was replaced.

### Editing the content
Each chapter's activities live in `content/chapter-NN.js` as plain lists of `[title, what to do]`, so they can be edited without touching the rest of the site.
